The problem

Small gaps that cost real time and money

01

The window closes quietly

Most major brands want registration within 60 days (Carrier: 90). Miss it and 10-year parts coverage can drop to 5.

02

Paperwork slips after the install

The tech is on to the next call, the office is on the phones, and the form never gets typed.

03

A failed part becomes your problem

A compressor part alone runs $500 to $2,000, and an unregistered unit is a harder conversation with the homeowner.

On most standard Trane, Lennox, Goodman and Rheem lines, a unit not registered within 60 days gets 5 years of parts coverage instead of 10 (Carrier: 90 days). Sources: Trane, Lennox, Goodman, Rheem, Carrier, verified Oct 20, 2026.
Exceptions: registration can't be required in CA, FL, GA (2026 installs on) or Quebec, and some premium lines (e.g. Lennox Signature) carry 10-year parts without registration. Sources: Goodman, Lennox warranty PDF.

FAQ

Straight answers

Who are you?

I'm Matt Breen, the founder. Unit Register is a small, new, independent service that I run myself, from New York. You deal with me directly.

What does it cost?

Your first 10 registrations are free, then simple flat pricing quoted upfront before you commit. No card needed to start.

What do you need from me?

A photo of each unit's data plate, the install date, and the homeowner's name and address, plus the homeowner's OK to register on their behalf.

Do you contact my customers?

I don't call or market to homeowners. Their details are used only on the manufacturer's registration form, and depending on the brand, the manufacturer may email the homeowner its own confirmation.

How do I opt out?

Reply "no" to any email from me and I won't email you again. You can also ask me to delete anything you've sent by writing to matt@unitregister.com, and I will.
